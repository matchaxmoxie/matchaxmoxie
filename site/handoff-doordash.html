This page is for the person who will run DoorDash orders after someone else set up the store.

Handoff means you can sign in, see a new order, and accept it without the person who set up the store sitting there.

Same stuck moment that turns into a support ticket. Short steps and a video so you do not have to ping the builder.

Steps

  1. Sign in to the Merchant Portal at merchants.doordash.com, or open the store tablet, with the store login.
  2. In the portal, open Orders. Active orders are in progress. Scheduled orders are for later. History is finished. On the tablet, a new order pops up.
  3. To accept an order on the tablet, tap the order, check the prep time, and tap Confirm. If Auto-confirm is on, the order is already accepted.
  4. The menu lives in the portal under Menu, then Menu Manager. Change an item there. Do not add a special or a price on this page.

If a label is a little different, stay in Orders for the tickets and in Menu for the items.

Write this down

  • The email that owns the merchant login.
  • The store name.
  • Who to call if an order will not clear.
  • Do not write the password on this page.
