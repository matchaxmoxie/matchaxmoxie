Six steps

  1. Go to wix.com and sign in. Pick the right site if you manage more than one.
  2. To edit the public site: open the dashboard, click Edit Site (or Design Site), change text or images, then Publish.
  3. For messages: open Inbox, read the thread, write a short reply, then Send.
  4. For store reviews: open Stores, then Product Reviews (or your reviews app), reply, then publish.
  5. To invite help: Settings → Roles & Permissions → Invite Collaborators. Give only the role they need.
  6. When someone leaves: Roles & Permissions → More Actions → Remove on that person.

Do not

  • Share the primary owner login with temporary contractors.
  • Publish major layout edits without Preview (and a mobile check).
  • Offer payment to delete a negative product review.
