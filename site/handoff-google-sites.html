Never write passwords on this page or on a printed sheet.

Six steps

  1. Open sites.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns the site.
  2. Click the site name to open it.
  3. Click Pages on the top right and pick the page you want to change.
  4. Click the text you want to update and type the new words.
  5. To replace a picture, click the image and choose Replace image.
  6. When it looks right, click Publish in the top right so visitors see the update.

Do not

  • Do not share owner passwords. Ask Jade before changing roles or billing.

When stuck

Wrong account, or you edited but forgot Publish. Stop and ask the owner before deleting pages.
