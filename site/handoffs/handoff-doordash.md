If POS is integrated, change hours in Square or Toast first.

## Six steps

1. Sign in at merchants.doordash.com (or open the store tablet) with the store login.
2. Open **Orders** to see active, scheduled, and history. On the tablet, confirm new orders.
3. Check store status and hours under merchant settings.
4. To add staff: **Settings → Manage Users → Add User** and send the invite.
5. When someone leaves, remove them from **Manage Users**.
6. If locked out, use official recovery / support. Do not invent a second merchant account.

## Do not

- Write the merchant password on this sheet.
- Leave former managers as Business Admin.
