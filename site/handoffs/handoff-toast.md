## Six steps

1. On the Toast terminal, sign in with your staff PIN or card. Owners use Toast Web for permissions.
2. Start the order, add items, tap **Pay**, then take the card on the reader.
3. Owners assign user access permissions in Toast Web with only what the job needs.
4. When someone leaves, remove or disable their employee access / PIN.
5. If DoorDash hours look wrong and Toast is linked, change hours in Toast first.
6. If permissions fail, check Toast troubleshooting before creating a second restaurant account.

## Do not

- Write staff PINs or passwords on this sheet.
- Share one manager login for every shift if individual employees exist.
