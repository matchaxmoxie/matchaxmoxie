If DoorDash is integrated with your POS, change hours in Square or Toast first so apps do not fight each other.

## Six steps

1. Sign in at squareup.com or open the Square Point of Sale app with your staff login.
2. Confirm you are on the right business location.
3. To take a payment: add items (or an amount), tap **Charge**, let the customer pay, wait for success.
4. Owner adds staff in Square Team / permissions tools with only the access they need.
5. When someone leaves, remove or disable their team permissions.
6. If DoorDash hours look wrong and POS is linked, change hours in Square first.

## Do not

- Write PINs or passwords on this sheet.
- Leave former staff with admin access.
