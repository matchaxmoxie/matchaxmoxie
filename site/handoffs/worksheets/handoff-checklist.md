## 2. Things I can do myself

- I can sign in using the correct family business account.
- I can update holiday or snow-day opening hours.
- I can replace a food photo on the website or Facebook.
- I can fix a typo in the menu.
- I can edit the homepage.
- I can save or publish changes.
- I can check the public page after publishing.
- I know who owns the domain.
- I know where to find platform support.

## 3. Things I should not change alone

- Do not change the domain or DNS without checking with Jade.
- Do not delete the website theme or start the site again.
- Do not install unknown plugins or give strangers administrator access.

## 4. When something goes wrong

**First:** Text or call Jade.

**Next:** Contact the relevant platform's official support team.

**Before asking for help:** Write down what you were trying to change and what happened. A screenshot can help, but hide private account information.

**Password reminder:** Passwords stay in the family password manager. Never write them on this worksheet, print them or send them in a message.

**Handoff complete when:** A family member can update hours, change a photo and correct menu wording without Jade doing the task for them.
