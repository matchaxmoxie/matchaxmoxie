## Responsibility check

- Every active account has a named long-term owner.
- Family members know which account to use for everyday edits.
- Domain and hosting renewal responsibilities are clear.
- Recovery access does not depend only on Jade's student account.
- Family members know when to contact Jade before making changes.
- Passwords and recovery codes remain in the family password manager.
