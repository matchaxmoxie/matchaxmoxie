## Six steps to manage the Page

1. **Open the restaurant Page.** Sign in using the authorised family account. Switch from your personal profile to the restaurant Page and check the name before editing.
2. **Update opening hours.** Find the Page information editing option for regular hours. For temporary holiday or snow-day changes, create a clear post explaining the new hours.
3. **Share a food photo.** Find the option to create a post, select the correct image, add a short description and look for Post or Publish.
4. **Share menu information.** Use a current, readable menu photo. Check prices, wording and opening hours before posting.
5. **Reply to comments.** Read the customer's question carefully. Keep replies short, friendly, all lowercase and without emojis. Ask Jade before responding to anything you are unsure about.
6. **Check your changes.** Return to the public Page. Confirm that the hours, photo, menu or reply appears correctly. If not, look for Save or Publish and check again.

## Do not

- Change Page roles or grant new administrator access without checking with Jade.
- Merge, delete or replace the restaurant Page.
- Connect unfamiliar apps or services to the Page.
