User Experience Designer

IUB Undergraduate Education
Aug 2025 to Dec 2025 · Bloomington, Indiana

Worked at the intersection of user experience (UX), privacy, and consumer technology.

  • Connected user research with broader questions around responsible technology design, in a field where research shows more than half of users fail to actually understand the privacy content they’re shown, even when they’ve read it (peer-reviewed UX privacy study, 2021).
  • Applied UX research to make technical privacy concepts clearer and easier to understand: 91% of people consent to terms and conditions without reading them, rising to 97% among 18 to 34 year olds (Deloitte).
  • Researched how users encounter and understand personal data collection, storage, and protection, where only 20% of consumers say tech providers are “very clear” about what data they collect and how it’s used (Deloitte, 2025).
  • Considered how interface and communication choices influence user trust and decision-making, since 82% of consumers have abandoned a brand over concerns about how their personal data was used (Thales Digital Trust Index, 2025).

Focus: User Experience, Data Privacy, Consumer Trust

Live Make: User Experience Designer · IUB Undergraduate Education · Desk tool: Plain Disclosure (zhao-langxi copy)

Business Technologist

Public Interest Technology University Network (PIT-UN)
May 2024 to Dec 2024 · 8 mos · Bloomington, Indiana

Researched how emerging AI (artificial intelligence) can move from technical possibility to technology that organisations can actually adopt.

  • Built an early foundation in AI product strategy and business technology that now informs my Digital Humans work.
  • Connected AI capabilities with organisational needs, implementation constraints, and long-term adoption, at a time when 92% of nonprofits now use AI in some capacity, yet only 7% describe their use as strategic enough to see measurable impact (Virtuous, 2026).
  • Evaluated how organisations might adopt emerging AI when technical capacity, resources, and trust are limited, given that larger nonprofits adopt AI at nearly twice the rate of smaller ones (66% versus 34%), and 47% of organisations operate with no AI policy at all (TechSoup, 2025).
  • Researched agentic AI and its potential applications in nonprofit environments.
  • Researched go-to-market (GTM) strategy for AI products in public-interest settings.

Focus: Agentic AI, AI Product Strategy, Nonprofit Technology, Go-to-Market Strategy, Business Technology, Organisational Adoption, Public-Interest Innovation

Web Developer

National Science Foundation (NSF)
Jun 2026 to Jul 2026 · 2 mos · Bloomington, Indiana

Contributed to a university project examining how consumer applications collect, store, and protect personal data, working with an industry partner under NDA.

  • Translated technical findings into clearer user-facing considerations around data and trust, at a moment when only 20% of consumers say tech providers are “very clear” about what data they collect or how it’s used (Deloitte, 2025).
  • Investigated consumer application data practices across collection, storage, and protection, informed by findings that 82.8% of iOS apps have been found to track private user data in some form (DataStackHub, 2025).
  • Contributed to web development and technical research connecting application behaviour with privacy considerations, in a landscape where 82% of consumers have abandoned a brand over how their data was used (Thales Digital Trust Index, 2025).
  • Worked within NDA requirements, keeping partner-specific technical and business information confidential.

Focus: Data Privacy, Consumer Trust, Application Security, Web Development, Technical Research, User Experience, Responsible Data Practices
