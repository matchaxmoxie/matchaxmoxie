User Experience at IUB Undergraduate Education

IUB Undergraduate Education
Aug 2025 to Dec 2025 · Bloomington, Indiana

UX and privacy at IUB Undergraduate Education. I helped make technical privacy ideas easier to read and use.

  • Ran user research on consent flows, settings screens, and fine print people skip.
  • Market context, not a flex: about 55% fail privacy comprehension even after reading (IEEE Access, 2021). Only 20% say providers are very clear on data use (Deloitte, 2025).
  • Tested plainer language and layout so trust matches what users actually understand.

Focus: User Experience, Data Privacy, Consumer Trust
