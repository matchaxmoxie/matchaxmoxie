Interaction design thinking

First formal UX course: move from vague problem to research-backed design outcomes, then defend them in a timed team presentation.

MW 3:00 to 4:20 Ballantine Hall 313

What the class was

I245 teaches how to think like an interaction designer before you worry about pixels: framing problems, gathering insights, and connecting research to prototypes and narratives. Syllabus reference: I245 Interaction Design Thinking (FA23), instructors Shunying An Blevis, PhD and associated instructor Kaustubh Salgaonkar.

What I did

  • Final showcase team: Team Mental Health (portfolio presentation block, November 2023).
  • Format: 7-minute presentation plus in-class peer feedback (slides, live demo, or video ... all under the time cap).
  • Emphasis: Final design outcomes tied to research findings and motivations, not a long process recap.
  • Canvas lab group label: Event Accessibility (separate from the final Mental Health team roster).

Skills I took forward

Structured critique, research-to-outcome storytelling, and presenting under a hard time limit ... all of which showed up again in I300 HCI, ServeIT client reviews, and MAP mentoring when students pitch projects.
