Interaction design research

The research half of the UX stack: theories of experience, how to collect and analyse UX data, ethics and sampling, then turn findings into journey maps, personas, scenarios, and design concepts.

Tue / Thu 4:45 to 6:05 Lindley Hall 101 Phil Jordan, PhD

What the class was

I345 sits after design thinking and alongside HCI practice: you are expected to document research rigorously and connect evidence to design moves. Canvas listed an overview of student work for the term; the capstone deliverable was the Field Trip Synthesis (final assignment graded before Thanksgiving 2024).

Pairs naturally with I300 HCI the same semester ... methods in 345, evaluation and team project in 300.

Methods & outputs

  • UX data collection and analysis (qualitative and quantitative framing from the syllabus).
  • Research ethics, sampling, and documentation habits.
  • Translation artifacts: experience journey maps, personas, scenarios, design concepts.
  • Field Trip Synthesis ... integrating observational / field research into a written synthesis (add PDF or excerpt when uploaded).

Why it matters for my portfolio

This is the course that makes “I like UX” defensible: you can point to how insights were gathered, not just what the mockup looks like. Same throughline as ServeIT discovery, Digital Humans trust questions, and MAP mentoring when students need a research plan before they code.
