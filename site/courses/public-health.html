Public health at population level

Three electives that sit next to my informatics degree: how stress shows up in daily life, how surveillance and surveys describe communities, and how chronic disease patterns are measured and explained ... not clinical care, not individual patient stories on this page.

SPH-H 180 SPH-E 250 SPH-E 353

Why this is in the classroom archive

Informatics is not only interfaces and code. It is also who counts in the data, what gets measured, and whether a number on a dashboard changes policy or behaviour. These courses gave me vocabulary for population health that shows up when I think about trust, disclosure, and who a system is for in UX and ServeIT work.

SPH-H 180 · Stress prevention and management

Term: Spring 2024

Foundational course on stress as a public health topic: prevention strategies, coping frameworks, and how stress interacts with health behaviours at a teachable, population-aware level.

Artifacts to add: one assignment title or reflection excerpt (no personal health narrative required).

SPH-E 250 · Public health surveillance and monitoring

Term: Spring 2025

Introduction to how public health systems monitor populations: data sources, indicators, and the logic behind surveillance ... the kind of thinking that pairs with informatics data work (cleaning, representing, and communicating counts without misleading people).

Artifacts to add: lab or project name from Canvas, one chart or table you are allowed to share (aggregate only).

SPH-E 353 · Distribution and determinants of chronic disease

Term: Fall 2025

Chronic conditions at population scale: who is affected, how patterns differ, and what social and structural factors show up in the literature and in class discussion. Canvas lab group theme: colorectal cancer screening context (course framing only ... add deliverable names when pasted from Canvas).

Artifacts to add: poster, slide deck, or written brief from the term (redact if needed). No participant or clinical field data.
