Why this is on an informatics portfolio

Digital Humans and ServeIT work both need business literacy: what a number means, who pays for a rollout, and whether a “smart” feature matches how a store actually runs. These classes are where I practised translating tech ideas into cost, strategy, and people problems ... the same muscle as GTM (go-to-market) thinking, without pretending I am a Kelley grad.

Synchronous Zoom structure, Connect homework, financial and managerial midterms online, in-person finals at Hodge Hall, and an end-of-semester project. My archived notes cover grading weights, participation drops, and exam prep ... the boring parts that keep you from guessing how the course actually works.

BUS-K 201 · The computer in business

Term: Spring 2025 (final pitch due 13 April 2025 on Canvas).

Team consultant brief: pitch a new AI-driven innovation for U.S. ALDI stores in a 4 to 6 minute video (YouTube link). Milestones walked from individual research through a full business pitch: problem definition, implementation plan, and a three-year cost-benefit projection with cited sources.

My local archive includes ALDI expansion and self-checkout loss research (market growth, Southeastern Grocers integration, shrink at self-checkout, retailer responses like item limits and computer vision). That research fed the “why this problem matters” layer before we argued for a specific tool.

Artifacts to add: unlisted YouTube link (if still up), slide deck export, and 2 sentences on the solution you pitched (no teammate names unless everyone agreed).

BUS-Z 302 · Managing and behaviour in organizations

Term: Fall 2026 · section 31488 · Prof. Richard J. Magjuka · Kinicki Organizational Behavior via McGraw Hill Connect.

Course objective (syllabus): build management literacy ... theories on leading people, individual and group behaviour, and HR practice, with honest self-assessment on management competencies. Modules on my Canvas map so far run from intro OB through values, individual differences, motivation, decision making, Predictably Irrational, and reliability / validity / quasi-experiments with an AI module thread.

Discussion prep and Connect SmartBook notes live in my private course folder; this page stays high level until I paste shareable discussion excerpts.

Artifacts to add: one discussion post you are proud of (values, Big Five, or motivation), plus optional link to a shareable study-notes page if I host one publicly later.
