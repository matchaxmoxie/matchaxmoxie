What ServeIT is

ServeIT is Indiana University's nonprofit technology clinic at Luddy. Student teams work with partners who may not have a full-time developer, building accessible websites and other technology around real needs.

The work is not just about finishing a demo. Someone needs to find information, complete a task, and maintain the site after the student team has moved on.

My ServeIT experience spans INFO-I 389 (internship) and INFO-I 489 (capstone) across multiple semesters.

What I did

I started as a freshman intern and moved into website team leadership. My role brought together client conversations, student teamwork, and the practical details of getting a project ready to hand over.

  • Intake and scope: Work out what can ship this semester and what should wait.
  • Delegation: Break work into tasks for teammates and keep responsibilities visible.
  • Accessibility and content: Check that the site and its handoff make sense for the people using it.
  • Communication: Align with mentors and clients through check-ins; kanban or Planner-style tracking for progress.
  • After the demo: Documentation and handoffs so partners can maintain the work without me.

Fall 2026: team lead for the Town of Nashville, Indiana ACC group (accessibility, security, and data visualisation in scope).
