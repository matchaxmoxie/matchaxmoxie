INFO-I 301 · Presentations for IT Professionals

Official title: Presentations for IT Professionals (section 14537, Fall 2025). On my Canvas it is nicknamed Career Prep because the whole point is professional presence, not slide tricks for their own sake.

Format: in-person open studio · Myles Brand Hall E130 · Mondays and Wednesdays 11:10 a.m. to 1:40 p.m. Grading: about 80% weekly presentations, 20% weekly assignments (presentation reviews, share-outs, feedback).

Syllabus learning outcomes I actually used:

  • Deliver presentations in more than one format (not only “death by PowerPoint”).
  • Build a narrative of my story and skills instead of listing every club once.
  • Track the evolution of how I talk about informatics, ServeIT, and business work in public.

I also keep IU’s Career Exploration and Student Employment shell on Canvas for appointments, fairs, and employment paperwork ... separate from I301, but part of the same “learn the system” stack.

Handshake · from user to Career Creator

Recruiting felt opaque when I started: profiles that read empty, internships that sounded mythical, and no one explaining what “networking” meant in practice. I fixed my own profile first, then started answering the questions first-year friends were too embarrassed to post in group chat.

IU partnered with Handshake early; I applied to their Career Creator programme and got accepted to post career-focused content for student audiences ... workplace norms, how to talk about small jobs and family businesses without sounding small, and how to use the platform without spamming recruiters.

The account grew to a large student following (six figures on the Handshake side) and serious profile traffic over time. That is not the flex. The flex is that strangers message me saying they finally uploaded a résumé or booked a career fair appointment.

I still mentor first-generation and low-income students on Handshake and LinkedIn: search filters, saved searches, profile photos that look like you on a good day, and how to turn ServeIT or restaurant work into bullets that scan.

LinkedIn and Instagram · same person, different rooms

LinkedIn is where I practise the hire-facing version of the story ... Digital Humans research, ServeIT ships, and business technology, with posts that read like advice to a friend who just got scared by a job description. I treat it as a live persona test: if the line sounds fake in the feed, it will sound fake in an interview.

Instagram · @zhao.langxi looks softer (film, matcha, campus life) but it feeds the same career arc. Photography taught me to document belonging ... the moments that make IU feel like home. Students trust career tips more when they have seen you as a whole human, not only a résumé screenshot carousel.

So yes: Handshake creator, LinkedIn posts, Instagram presence ... one loop. Class forced weekly reps; platforms forced me to keep the story honest after the semester ended.
