Property management system

Semester client build: connect local homeowners with college students seeking housing ... from requirements and personas through ERD, SQL, and a web portal (SPECS grading, 13 project steps).

Problem framing

Team 39 scoped a property management system for the student rental market: owners list space, students search and apply, managers coordinate communication. Project Step 2 documented three personas (student seeker, homeowner, professional manager) and wireframes aligned to those journeys.

  • Lilly Bloom ... college student housing seeker.
  • Robert Chen ... local homeowner with rental inventory.
  • Sarah Martinez ... property manager facilitating matches.

Wireframe and persona boards lived in Figma (linked in the Step 2 submission). Local ERD source: ERD i308 39.drawio.

How the class worked

Kate Muehlhaus and Dan Pierz lectured; UI mentors graded weekly specs. Deliverables: in-class team notes, alternating individual/team homework (8/9 specs = pass), 12 graded project steps, weekly teamwork and rotating team lead, final presentations week 12 (no week-13 exam).

Separate drills (e.g. BandData SQL homework) practised joins and constraints on sample music data before the client schema hardened.
