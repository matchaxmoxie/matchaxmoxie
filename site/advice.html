soft college advice

IU Bloomington 🍪 · manifesting

Not a syllabus. Not a blueprint. MANIFESTING fuel for who you are becoming ... honest lines for any campus. Make college what you make of it ... and make more of it than just college.

the core

  1. There is no single blueprint for college. You make it what you make of it.
  2. Same school does not mean the same experience. Your chapter is yours.
  3. Roommate paths vary ... chosen, random, alone, or with friends. Alone can feel lonely early on. Living with people teaches you things either way.
  4. Location and community can matter more than the prettiest dorm. Adjustable beats perfect on paper.
  5. First semester is for testing routines until yours clicks. Hyperproductive weeks and slow weeks are both allowed.
  6. Check in with yourself at the one-month mark. How you actually feel beats the highlight reel.
  7. Feeling put together in an outfit or hair that feels like you helps you leave the room and join campus life.
  8. Friend groups shift after welcome week. Be yourself. You will find your people.
  9. FOMO is not real. Stay in to study, rest, or because you want to. Find people who respect that.
  10. Do not panic about your major freshman year. Switching when something fits you better is OK.
  11. Hard or dreaded classes can become favourites. Self-motivation matters in big lectures.
  12. Transferring is valid courage, not giving up. Happy-now and sad-what-if can sit together.
  13. Saying bye to a chapter can be exciting and genuinely sad at the same time. Both can be true.
  14. Hobbies alone ... cook, create, knit, walk ... are anchors on hard days.
  15. Value friendships and your own pace over forcing a relationship rulebook. Single seasons can be special too.
  16. Struggles can leave you stronger without romanticising the pain.

manifesting

  1. Make more of college than just college ... classes plus creative work plus a real life you actually like.
  2. If you love what you are building, you find time to balance school, projects, and people.
  3. Flexible schedules and being on campus both count. Your college experience is still yours.
  4. Coming back after time away still counts. Online weeks still count. Presence is not one shape.
  5. Productive Mondays. New-chapter energy. Go after what you want while you are still in school.
  6. Design your week around your energy ... busy days and free days can both be intentional.
  7. Ghost-mode grind weeks for finals are allowed. Soft reset weeks after are also allowed.
  8. Anxiety before an event is normal. Feeling glad you went afterward is also normal.
  9. Present-moment fall vibes ... this season will not loop back the same way.
  10. Romanticise your season even if the weather does not cooperate. Make the festive parts yourself.
  11. When a hot streak breaks, restart without self-hate. Pick the camera / project / week back up.

week rhythm

  • A new month can be a soft reset ... get your life together and take it easy in the same breath.
  • Self-care weekends matter as much as going-out weekends. You do not owe entertainment every day.
  • Campus resources are for using ... tutors, quiet rooms, office hours. Getting help is a strategy.
  • Midterms and life moves can stack. Overwhelm is allowed. One bag, one desk, one day at a time.
  • Game day / big campus nights are optional belonging. Go if you want. Leave on your own clock.
  • Time in college is weird ... forever and yesterday at once. That is why present-moment matters.

also true

  • Be present. This moment will not return.
  • Rest when your body is down. Quiet study rooms beat forcing a cough through the library.
  • Go out when the mood hits. Leave on your own clock.
  • Less proving yourself online = more peace.
  • Milestones away from home can feel strange. Still yours to celebrate your way.
  • Style can be continuity through hard years ... wear what feels like the next version of you.
