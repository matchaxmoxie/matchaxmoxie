soft college advice

IU Bloomington 🍪

Soft college honesty for any campus. You are still figuring it out ... and that is allowed.

no right or wrong way

  1. There is no right or wrong way to do college. You make it what you make of it.
  2. Same school does not mean the same experience. Different schools are different shapes of college. Your path is yours.
  3. Let go of the picture-perfect script ... the friend group that never shifts, the party every weekend, perfect grades, president of everything. That movie is not required.
  4. The “best four years of your life” pressure is real. You can release it. College can be meaningful without being a highlight reel every day.
  5. You are still figuring it out. You do not need everything together to belong here.
  6. What you are handed matters. What you do with it matters more.

change your mind

  1. Allow yourself to change your mind. Fear not growing more than fear of looking inconsistent.
  2. Timelines differ. Majors can change. Say yes to what excites you now.
  3. You cannot “get college wrong” by choosing, then leaving, transferring, or reshaping senior year. Growth is not a mistake.
  4. Hard or dreaded classes can become favourites. Self-motivation matters when the room is big.
  5. Transferring is valid courage, not giving up. Happy-now and sad-what-if can sit together.
  6. Plans change ... campus pics become a garden walk, a venue says no, the week pivots. That is still your story.

comparison and FOMO

  1. Comparison steals joy. Highlight reels are not the full story.
  2. FOMO is often protection. Stay in to study, rest, or because you want to. Find people who respect that.
  3. OK if nightlife or college-town chaos is not your joy. There are other ways to make memories. Find your wavelength.
  4. Friend groups shift after welcome week. Be yourself. You will find your people.
  5. Roommate paths vary ... chosen, random, alone, or with friends. Alone can feel lonely early on. Living with people teaches you things either way.
  6. Location and community can matter more than the prettiest dorm. Adjustable beats perfect on paper.

hard seasons

  1. When the year does not match the picture, that does not mean you failed. Hard freshman seasons happen. Things can get better if you let them.
  2. Treat hard seasons as stepping stones. Zoom out. Struggles can leave you stronger without romanticising the pain.
  3. Gratitude over self-pity. Acknowledge what is hard without living as a victim of it.
  4. You are not behind. You are not in the wrong place if you are still becoming.
  5. Even not-as-planned can mean life working in your favour. Everything that happened can lead here.
  6. Nostalgia means you had a life worth loving and missing. Freshman-you vs now ... times change. That can feel perfect.

show up soft

  1. This chapter belongs to you. Collect small wins and glimmers.
  2. Show up for yourself in soft ways ... tea, a study Saturday, a walk ... when the loud version is not you.
  3. First semester is for testing routines until yours clicks. Hyperproductive weeks and slow weeks are both allowed.
  4. Check in with yourself at the one-month mark. How you actually feel beats the highlight reel.
  5. Feeling put together in an outfit or hair that feels like you helps you leave the room and join campus life.
  6. Hobbies alone ... cook, create, knit, walk ... are anchors on hard days.
  7. Value friendships and your own pace over forcing a relationship rulebook. Single seasons can be special too.
  8. You do not need it all figured out before you start living it.
