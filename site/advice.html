soft college advice

IU Bloomington 🍪 · manifesting

Not a syllabus. Not a blueprint. Fuel for who you are becoming ... honest lines for any campus. Make college what you make of it.

the core

  1. There is no single blueprint for college. You make it what you make of it.
  2. Same school does not mean the same experience. Your chapter is yours.
  3. Roommate paths vary ... chosen, random, alone, or with friends. Alone can feel lonely early on. Living with people teaches you things either way.
  4. First semester is for testing routines until yours clicks. Hyperproductive weeks and slow weeks are both allowed.
  5. Feeling put together in an outfit or hair that feels like you helps you leave the room and join campus life.
  6. Friend groups shift after welcome week. Be yourself. You will find your people.
  7. FOMO is not real. Stay in to study, rest, or because you want to. Find people who respect that.
  8. Do not panic about your major freshman year. Switching when something fits you better is OK.
  9. Hard or dreaded classes can become favourites. Self-motivation matters in big lectures.
  10. Transferring is valid courage, not giving up. Happy-now and sad-what-if can sit together.
  11. Hobbies alone ... cook, create, knit, walk ... are anchors on hard days.
  12. Value friendships and your own pace over forcing a relationship rulebook.
  13. Struggles can leave you stronger without romanticising the pain.

also true

  • Be present. This moment will not return.
  • Rest when your body is down. Quiet study rooms beat forcing a cough through the library.
  • Go out when the mood hits. Leave on your own clock.
  • Less proving yourself online = more peace.
