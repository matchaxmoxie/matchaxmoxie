The organisation website's content-management system (WordPress.com or self-hosted).

Who owns the login?

  • **Flavour:** WordPress.com **or** self-hosted (circle one on paper copy)
  • **Site owner / admin:** _________________________________
  • **Hosting company (if self-hosted):** _________________________________
  • **Domain owner:** _________________________________

Never write passwords on this page or on a printed sheet.

Six steps

  1. Confirm whether the site is WordPress.com or self-hosted (`/wp-admin`).
  2. Sign in with your own account. Open **Users** to see who has access.
  3. Add people with the lowest role that fits the job (Author or Editor often beats Administrator).
  4. On **Add New User**, leave the password-email checkbox unchecked; they set their own password.
  5. When someone leaves, remove or demote their user account.
  6. If you forgot the password, use the official reset for WordPress.com or your host.

Do not

  • Share one Administrator password across the team.
  • Email passwords in plain text.
