What IU Informatics is (2023 to 2027)

At Luddy, the B.S. in Informatics is a 120-credit degree: informatics core, electives, and a cognate (specialisation track), plus room for minors. You learn how people, organisations, and software meet ... design, data, ethics, and implementation, not just one language.

Official framing: IU Academic Bulletin · Informatics B.S.. Thread on these notes: interaction design / HCI business technology public health nonprofit and city tech (ServeIT) career platforms (Handshake · LinkedIn).
