the vibe

  • No single blueprint. Same school ≠ same experience. Your chapter is yours.
  • FOMO is not real. Stay in to study, rest, or because you want to.
  • Be yourself. Friend groups shift. You will find your people.
  • Transferring is courage, not giving up. Happy-now and sad-what-if can sit together.
  • Rest when your body is down. Hobbies alone are anchors on hard days.
  • Be present. This moment will not return.
