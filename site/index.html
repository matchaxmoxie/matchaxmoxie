    Why clear next steps matter

    National U.S. numbers (not IU-specific). First-generation and low-income students often face steeper gaps early. Clear next steps and real mentors help close what the brochure leaves blank.

    • 20% vs 42%

      Among 2002 U.S. high school sophomores who later enrolled in college, 20% of first-generation students had earned a bachelor’s degree by 2012, compared with 42% of students whose parent held a bachelor’s or higher. (Source)

    • 33% vs 14%

      Three years after first enrolling (2003 to 04 cohort), 33% of first-generation students had left postsecondary education without a credential, compared with 14% of peers whose parents held a bachelor’s. (Source)

    • 47% vs 58%

      Among students who entered four-year institutions in 2014 to 15, Pell Grant recipients had a 47% eight-year completion rate, compared with 58% for nonrecipients. (Source)
