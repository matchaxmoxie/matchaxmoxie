who runs this room

I’m Jade.

I’m an Informatics student at IU, STEM Peer Mentor / MAP Coordinator with Faculty and Staff for Student Excellence (FASE), and a Programme Assistant with the Hudson & Holland Scholars Programme (HHSP).

I’ve spent a lot of time learning how IU works ... and also learning that there is a big difference between information being technically available and information actually being useful.

That’s what this site is for.

I make guides for the questions students actually ask:

  • Where do I start?
  • What am I supposed to do next?
  • Who do I ask?
  • Is this actually required?
  • What happens if I have no idea what I’m doing?

The goal is not to make college look perfectly organized. It’s to make the next step easier to see.

And yes ... I grew up working in a family restaurant, so apparently my preferred organizational system is still “make sure everyone knows where the next thing is.”

Welcome to the classroom

I’m Jade ... an Informatics student at Indiana University, STEM Peer Mentor / MAP Coordinator with FASE, and someone who has spent enough time navigating IU to know that the hardest part is sometimes just figuring out what the next step is.

matchaxmoxie is my student-facing space for that.

Here you’ll find:

  • your path A six-step guide for getting oriented, making decisions, and moving through IU without feeling like you need the entire four-year plan figured out on day one.

  • footprint Small reflections on what we leave behind ... on campus, in classrooms, in organizations, and in the people we meet.
