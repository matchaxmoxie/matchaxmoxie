Quick start

  1. What this is: a local 21+ cheat sheet for the strip, not a hire page.
  2. What to do next: skim the strip cards, then pick a night from the weekly rundown.
  3. Sources: hours and deals change · check the venue before you go.

Last verified · Spring 2025 student notes. Specials move. Ask a friend who was there last week.

the strip

  • Kok

    Kilroy’s on Kirkwood

    Address
    502 E Kirkwood
    Vibe
    The default. Patio out back.
    Tip
    Say it like a local: “you going to Kok?”

  • Sports

    Kilroy’s Sports

    Address
    319 N Walnut
    Vibe
    Five rooms: Jungle, Atrium, main, rooftop, ice bar. Bigger energy, not the default.
    Tip
    Thu–Sat main ~9pm–3am when you want the rooms, not every night.

  • Brothers

    Address
    Kirkwood strip · near the walk
    Vibe
    Casual food-first stop on the crawl.
    Tip
    $3 burger Tuesdays when the deal is on.

  • Stairs

    Upstairs Pub

    Address
    Kirkwood strip · look for the stairs
    Vibe
    Starts chill, turns dance-y late.
    Tip
    Show later if you want the dance floor, not the early quiet.

  • Dunnkirk

    Address
    Speakeasy behind Stairs
    Vibe
    Cocktails / date night · quieter than the strip default.
    Tip
    Say it like you’ve been. Cred lives here.

  • Yogi’s

    Address
    Kirkwood strip
    Vibe
    Screens everywhere · game-day energy.
    Tip
    Go when you want a game on, not a quiet talk.

  • Louie’s

    Buffalouie’s · the Gables

    Address
    The Gables · near Stairs
    Vibe
    Wings first, bar second.
    Tip
    Best wings on this list · order before the late rush.

  • The Vault

    Address
    Inside the IMU
    Vibe
    Campus bar · easy meet-up before the strip.
    Tip
    Use it when you’re already on campus and want a short hop.

games / chill

  • The Vid

    Video Saloon

    Address
    South of the main strip · walkable
    Vibe
    Dive · pool · darts · cheap pitchers.
    Tip
    End here when you want games over dancing.

  • The Cade

    Address
    Near Kirkwood · arcade bar
    Vibe
    Arcade bar · Mario Kart energy · 21+.
    Tip
    Good mid-route stop between food and the late strip.

  • Nick’s

    English Hut · since 1927

    Address
    423 E Kirkwood
    Vibe
    Classic b-town · food + pint, not a dance floor.
    Tip
    Order a sink the biz · biz fries upstairs · don’t overthink it.

craft / grown-up

  • The Tap

    Address
    Downtown Bloomington
    Vibe
    Beer-first · 400+ taps, brews its own.
    Tip
    Ask for a house pour if the board feels huge.

  • Crazy Horse

    Address
    Kirkwood · locals dive
    Vibe
    Locals dive · plaque-challenge energy.
    Tip
    80 beers plaque challenge if you like a slow grind.

  • Upland

    Address
    Near campus · OG Bloomington brewery
    Vibe
    Brewery sit-down · slower night.
    Tip
    Pick this when you want conversation over the strip crawl.

  • Irish Lion

    Address
    Historic Irish pub on Kirkwood
    Vibe
    Pub food + quieter tables than Kok.
    Tip
    Good early dinner before you decide on the strip.

cocktails / date

  • Dunnkirk

    Address
    Behind Stairs
    Vibe
    Date-night cocktails · same speakeasy as above.
    Tip
    Book the quieter room energy before the strip gets loud.

  • Serendipity

    Address
    Downtown · cocktail bar
    Vibe
    Martini energy · dressier than the strip default.
    Tip
    Go for one good drink, not a full crawl.

  • C3 · Alchemy · Orbit Room

    Address
    Downtown cluster · Orbit is the easy landmark
    Vibe
    Cocktail / late-bite mix · Orbit: pinball + gourmet hot dogs.
    Tip
    Orbit when you want a snack and a game between bars.

music / dance

  • The Bird

    Bluebird

    Address
    Walnut · real venue
    Vibe
    Real venue · three bars · lines move.
    Tip
    Only if the band’s good · check the night before you commit.

  • Root Cellar

    Address
    Downtown · club-leaning
    Vibe
    Most club-like · karaoke nights.
    Tip
    Show when you want dance floor, not a patio chat.

  • Bishop / Blockhouse

    Address
    Under the Back Door
    Vibe
    Downstairs music rooms · honky tonk Tuesdays.
    Tip
    Tuesday honky tonk if that’s your mood.

  • Buskirk-Chumley

    Address
    Historic theater on Kirkwood
    Vibe
    Historic theater · not a bar.
    Tip
    Shows and tickets, not a crawl stop. Listed so you don’t confuse it with the strip bars.

townie cred

  • The Alley

    Alley Bar

    Address
    Downtown alley · ask a local
    Vibe
    Townie-leaning · less first-year default.
    Tip
    Go with someone who already knows the door.

  • Atlas

    Atlas Ballroom

    Address
    Downtown · ballroom vibe
    Vibe
    Skee-ball · photo booth · playful night.
    Tip
    Good when you want games without The Cade’s arcade density.

  • off-strip locals

    Address
    Scattered off the main Kirkwood crawl
    Vibe
    Sleeper’s · Sinkhole · Hoppy Wobbles · Office Lounge.
    Tip
    Ask a townie which one fits the night · don’t force all four.

  • The Back Door

    Address
    Downtown · Bloomington’s queer bar
    Vibe
    Drag · dance · beloved.
    Tip
    Show up for the community night, not as a drive-by photo stop.

weekly soft rundown

  1. Mon · evening — quiet · rest or early food
  2. Tue · ~9pm — $2 Stairs (or Sports / Kok)
  3. Wed · night — Bird · dollar beers · bands
  4. Thu · ~9pm — Sports
  5. Fri · late — Stairs / The Vid
  6. Sat · late — Stairs · Kok into Vid
  7. Sun · daytime / evening — rest · sometimes football @ Sports

Little 500 weekend in April ... strip goes off early.

one soft route

  1. ~5pm — Nick’s · sink the biz
  2. early evening — Brothers if there’s a food deal
  3. mid-route — Cade as a stop between food and late
  4. only if the bill’s good — Bird
  5. toward close — Kok + Stairs
  6. end — Vid or food

Sports for moods / events, not every night.
