0 of 6

You completed step 0 of 6.

  1. Step 1 · Find your footing You do not need a four-year plan today. Curious about code now? Try AP CSP (Advanced Placement Computer Science Principles) if your school offers it. I took it as a high school freshman. Never too early. AP notes on the IU guide.
  2. Step 2 · Learn the map Find the official academic and campus resources. Prefer university pages over any tip sheet (including mine): Luddy bulletin (degree rules), AP credit table, Registrar calendar for FA26 to FA27, First Year Experience (FYE = first-year orientation and support hub), and student mental health (link-out only · not counseling from me). Official links list.
  3. Step 3 · Try something Class, club, project, job ... something small counts. Archival · Year ’23 (2023) Packing, dorms, Welcome Week vibes, or a Scratch starter. Verify hall rules and dates on live IU pages. Campus survival archive · Scratch studio.
  4. Step 4 · Find your people Mentors, classmates, faculty, staff. Peek how years stack: freshman · sophomore · junior · Madrid · senior. Your people show up across those years.
  5. Step 5 · Make a footprint Build something, help someone, contribute somewhere. footprint is the dare list for that ... not a resume trail.
  6. Step 6 · Keep going Your path can change. When you need careers later: learn about yourself, explore options, Luddy career offices. Peek the slides on the IU guide · career strip and Handshake.
