What Handshake is for

At IU Bloomington, Handshake is the campus hub for student employment and early career moves. Employers on it are looking for IU students specifically. In plain terms, students use it to:

  • Jobs and internships ... on-campus and off-campus postings, part-time work, summer roles.
  • Campus and work-study style roles ... student jobs that live on the same board as external listings.
  • Events and fairs ... career fairs, info sessions, workshops (filter by Events).
  • Career coaching ... book Walter Center (College) or school career appointments via Career Center → Appointments. Luddy / Kelley / other schools may route to their own coaches in the same menu.
  • Employer radar ... when visibility is open, recruiters can find and message students whose skills match.

Official IU notes (Walter Center / Student Employment): log in at the Bloomington URL above; visibility Private / Employers / Community is a real choice, not a formality.

Profile checklist (first-gen friendly)

Empty profiles don’t show up well. Employers often search work experience, skills, and clubs before they message anyone. Walk through this once together:

  • Visibility: Private is often the default ... employers can’t find or message them. Switch to Employers or Community if they want outreach. Private still lets them browse and apply; it just hides them from recruiter search.
  • Résumé: upload a current PDF, check auto-fill, fix weird lines. One clean page beats a fancy blank.
  • Skills: add 5 to 8 real ones (tools, languages, customer service, lab software ... whatever is true). Fake skills get caught in interviews.
  • Experience counts broadly: paid job, work-study, volunteer, research, family business, class project with a real deliverable, or a club officer role. One honest row beats a blank Experience section.
  • Education / grad year: credit-hour auto-fill lies sometimes. Edit graduation date by hand.
  • Interests (optional): job type, location, and role preferences improve recommendations. Skip if they’re overwhelmed; come back later.
  • Can’t apply: usually missing résumé or incomplete required fields. Fix profile first, then retry the posting.

Search and apply habits

  • Quality over spray: one posting they actually read beats twenty empty Quick Apply clicks. Recruiters notice copy-paste applications.
  • Save a search: Jobs → set filters (keyword, location, job type) → save. Revisit weekly instead of starting from zero.
  • Follow 1 to 2 employers they care about (Employers → Follow). Not twenty. Depth beats noise.
  • Read the posting: deadline, location, documents required, “must be enrolled” lines. Ask before they hit Apply if anything looks off.
  • Login weirdness: Bloomington URL + IU SSO. Chrome / Firefox / private window. Escalate if SSO still fails ... don’t invent a workaround.

Soft career advice (say out loud)

It’s OK not to know the “hidden curriculum.” First-generation students often weren’t told that résumés get rewritten, that cold-applying is normal, or that career centres exist for them. Normalise asking.
  • One good application beats twenty empty ones. Tailor the résumé bullet or cover note to that posting. Time spent reading beats time spent clicking.
  • Talk to Walter Center / school career services. Book in Handshake (Career Center → Appointments). Drop-ins help with login and profile; coaches help with direction, résumé rewrite, and interview practice.
  • Ask mentors and peers where they found their first campus job. Names and “I applied here” stories beat generic advice.
  • Campus jobs count. Front desk, dining, tutoring, lab help ... they’re real experience and often friendlier entry points than a brand-name internship on day one.
  • You don’t need a perfect plan. A next step (upload résumé, follow one employer, apply to one role) is enough for one drop-in.

When to escalate

  • Missed Handshake 101 / makeup rules: get them unstuck on the tool today. Programme requirements stay with 21st CS / Mentoring Services. Ask Dhakir Abdullah for programme questions (per Dr. Smith’s note).
  • Full résumé rewrite or career direction: bigger than a drop-in ... book Walter Center or their school career office in Handshake.
  • Advising / enrolment / scholarship holds: academic advisors or 21st CS coaches, not Handshake troubleshooting.
  • SSO still broken after a clean browser try: escalate to IU tech / UITS channels they already use ... don’t invent a password reset story.
  • Unclear on the walkthrough yourself? Email Jade ... happy to do Handshake for 10 minutes.
