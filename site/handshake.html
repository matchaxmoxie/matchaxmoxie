Handshake prep for mentors

Short pack for drop-ins when 21st Century Scholars miss Handshake 101. Functional tips only. Not a replacement for the official session.

Why

Dr. Smith’s note: 21st Century Scholars who miss Handshake 101 may come to drop-in hours. If you’ve used Handshake before (or sat through last year’s FASE prep), poke around again for 15 minutes so you’re ready.

We’re not replacing Handshake 101. Don’t invent rooms or times ... send them back to their 21st CS / Mentoring Services emails for that. Short help when someone is stuck.

A lot of first-generation students have never used a campus job board. Say it plainly: IU’s place for jobs and internships. Log in with your IU account, fix the profile so employers can see you, then search and apply. One goal per visit is enough.
IU Bloomington Handshake
https://iubloomington.joinhandshake.com
IU username + passphrase. Chrome or Firefox. Bloomington only ... not Gmail signup, not Indianapolis.

Functional tips

  • Visibility: Private is often the default ... employers can’t find or message them. Switch to Employers or Community if they want outreach. Empty profiles don’t show up well.
  • Résumé: upload a current PDF, check auto-fill, fix weird lines. Add 5 to 8 real skills. One job, volunteer, or club row beats a blank Experience section.
  • Don’t spray Quick Apply: one posting they actually read beats a pile of spam clicks. Save the search. Follow 1 to 2 employers they care about.
  • Save searches / follow: Jobs → filters → save. Employers → Follow.
  • Grad year wrong: credit-hour auto-fill lies sometimes. Edit education / graduation date by hand.
  • Can’t apply: usually missing résumé or incomplete required fields. Fix profile first.
  • Login weirdness: Bloomington URL + IU SSO. Chrome / Firefox / private window. Escalate if SSO still fails ... don’t invent a workaround.
  • Bigger than a drop-in: full résumé rewrite or career direction → book Walter Center / school career appointment in Handshake (Career Center → Appointments).
  • Missed Handshake 101: get them unstuck today. Makeup rules stay with 21st CS / Mentoring Services. Ask Dhakir Abdullah for programme questions.

AdRx

After help, log in AdRx. Short and factual: who, when, what, next step. If you’re unsure how your unit wants these coded, ask before inventing a note style.
