Be here for this minute. Soak the week you are in ... friendship, seasons, matcha mornings.

right now

  • Today, not yesterday, not tomorrow. The past is over. The future is uncertain ... stop living ahead of yourself.
  • Right place, right time. Trust that for a minute.
  • Soak every moment, good or hard. You never get this exact minute back.
  • In a year you will reminisce on now. Anything in your life can change ... so be here.
  • Nostalgia means you had a life worth loving and missing. That is lucky.
  • All we got is right now. Best vibes are high vibes.
  • Soak the moment. Stop rushing everything.

week and season

  • Happy Monday energy. Freezing outside? Library and get started on the week anyway.
  • When classes finally feel major-focused, let yourself get excited about depth.
  • Character development season ... all-over-the-place thoughts are OK. Deep thinking is allowed.
  • Inspiration comes when it comes. Pick the creative work back up without self-hate.
  • Make a drink and head out. Let your feet take you. Magical ordinary day.
  • Split days on purpose so they do not blur into one loop.
  • Get school done early so the fun week is not worried.
  • Mid-fall contentment. The plot thickens. Claim the season even without cold weather.
  • New month on a Monday = soft kickstart. Month of magic, love, happiness, opportunity.
  • Sleep early when you have been lacking it. Replenish after a hectic day.
  • Busy weeks without going too crazy if you are easily overstimulated ... keep it on the soft deal.
  • Finish the semester with your best foot forward. Absorb the first real break in a long time.
  • End-of-term soak: few weeks left, show up for it.
  • Halloween pressure: OK not to go big. Soft nights count.
  • Make life magical. Midweek reset. Be kind. Day is our oyster.
