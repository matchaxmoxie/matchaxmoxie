What this Informatics year was about

Fall locked in Human-Computer Interaction Design and Programming (INFO-I 300 · INFO = Informatics), Interaction Design Research (INFO-I 345), and mathematical foundations (INFO-I 201). Spring added information representation (INFO-I 308) plus Kelley-side accounting and computing-in-business: Business & Society cognate in motion. ServeIT (IU’s nonprofit tech clinic) stayed on the schedule both semesters (INFO-I 389).

Design research methods, HCI build muscle, electives across campus, and sites for Bloomington nonprofit partners. Website team lead work on jadexzhao (龙 · dragon) grew alongside these credits.
