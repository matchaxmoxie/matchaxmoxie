Why support tickets pile up

Support tickets spike when the builder leaves and staff cannot find how to edit a page, post, reply to a review, take a payment, or publish.

People open tickets for the same five tasks over and over because nobody left a short path with proof.

These handoffs are the fix: ELI5 steps plus a video, so the next person can do it without pinging Jade.
