For the next person who edits the live Squarespace site without a full tech background.

Who owns the login?

  • **Email that owns Squarespace:** _________________________________
  • **Live site address:** _________________________________
  • **Backup contact:** _________________________________
  • **Password manager location:** _________________________________

Never write passwords on this page or on a printed sheet.

Six steps

  1. Go to squarespace.com and sign in with the email that owns the site.
  2. Open the site and click **Pages**.
  3. Choose the page you want to edit.
  4. Click **Edit** and change the text or pictures.
  5. When you are done, click **Done**, then **Save**.
  6. If the page is still a draft, click **Publish** so the public site updates.

Do not

  • Do not share owner passwords. Ask Jade before changing roles or billing.

When stuck

A draft that was never published is the most common issue. Check **Publish** before calling support.
