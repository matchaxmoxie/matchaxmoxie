What an Informatics degree actually covers

At Luddy, the B.S. in Informatics is a 120-credit undergraduate degree: an informatics core, electives, and a cognate (a specialisation track), plus room for minors. You study how people, organisations, and software meet ... design, data, ethics, and implementation, not a single language.

That is the official shape. This notebook’s thread happens to be interaction design / HCI, business technology, public health, nonprofit and city tech (ServeIT), and career platforms. Someone else’s cognate will look different.

This page is not academic advising. Requirements change. Read the bulletin, then ask a real advisor.

Source: IU Academic Bulletin · Informatics B.S. (2024 to 2025).
