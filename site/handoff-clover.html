For the next person who rings a sale and takes a card payment on Clover.

Who owns the login?

  • **Clover account owner (clover.com):** _________________________________
  • **Who gives out the device passcode:** _________________________________
  • **Device or location name:** _________________________________

Never write passwords on this page or on a printed sheet.

Six steps

  1. On the Clover device, sign in with the passcode your manager gave you.
  2. Open **Register** and tap the items or type the amount.
  3. Tap **Pay** or **Charge**.
  4. The customer inserts, swipes, or taps their card.
  5. Choose a receipt method.
  6. Tap **Done** and confirm the sale cleared.

Do not

  • Do not share owner passwords. Ask Jade before changing roles or billing.
